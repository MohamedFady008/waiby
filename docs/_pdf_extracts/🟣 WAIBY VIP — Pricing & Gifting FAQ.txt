Duration  Price

90  days  60  Buds  (available  at  higher  VIP  levels)  
VIP  Time  is  stackable  and  can  be  extended  anytime.

Can  other  people  gift  me  VIP?  
Yes.  
VIP  Time  can  be  gifted  by  other  users ,  such  as  customers  gifting  VIP  to  creators.  
VIP  gifting  rules  
●  VIP  gifts  are  purchased  with  Buds  only  
 ●  Gifted  VIP  costs  slightly  more  than  self-purchase  
 ●  Gifted  VIP  Time  activates  immediately  
 ●  Buds  earned  from  rewards  cannot  be  used  for  gifting  
 
Gift  VIP  pricing  (Buds)  
Duration  Price  
7  days  14  Buds  
30  days  ⭐  32  Buds  
90  days  80  Buds  (VIP  customers  only)   
Can  Gems  be  used  to  gift  VIP?  
❌  No.  
Gifting  VIP  is  intentionally  limited  to  Buds  to  prevent  abuse  and  maintain  fairness.  
 
Can  I  get  VIP  for  free?  
Sometimes,  yes.  
VIP  Time  may  be  granted  temporarily  through:  
●  Events  
 ●  Promotions  
 ●  Limited  rewards  
 
Free  VIP  Time:  
●  Is  usually  24  hours  
 ●  Cannot  be  stacked  with  itself  
 ●  Is  meant  as  a  trial  or  preview  
 
 
What  happens  if  my  VIP  Time  expires?  
Nothing  is  lost.  
●  Your  VIP  Level  stays  
 ●  Your  perks  are  paused  
 ●  Visual  features  fall  back  to  basic  versions  
 
Reactivating  VIP  Time  restores  everything  instantly.  
 
Does  VIP  affect  earnings  or  ranking?  
No.  
VIP  does  not :  
●  Increase  payouts  
 ●  Reduce  fees  
 ●  Guarantee  ranking  or  exposure  
 
VIP  is  cosmetic,  expressive,  and  tool-based  —  not  pay-to-win .
