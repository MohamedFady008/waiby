1.  Platform  Role  in  Payments  
WAIBY  operates  as  a  managed  marketplace  and  acts  as  the  commercial  intermediary  for  
all
 
transactions
 
processed
 
on
 
the
 
platform.
 
●  Customers  pay  WAIBY  directly  for  services,  subscriptions,  and  digital  interactions.  
 ●  WAIBY  collects  and  processes  all  customer  payments.  
 ●  WAIBY  later  pays  creators  their  earnings,  net  of  applicable  platform  fees,  holds,  
and
 
adjustments
.
 
 
This  structure  ensures:  
●  Clear  accountability  for  customers  
 ●  Centralized  compliance  and  invoicing  
 ●  Reduced  fraud  and  chargeback  exposure  
 
 
2.  Base  Currency  &  Accounting  Model  
2.1  Single  Source  of  Truth  
WAIBY  operates  internally  exclusively  in  USD  (United  States  Dollars) .  
All  internal  records  —  including  balances,  orders,  subscriptions,  fees,  refunds,  and  payouts  
—
 
are
 
denominated
 
and
 
reconciled
 
in
 
USD.
 
2.2  External  Currency  Conversion  
Customers  may  pay  using  other  currencies  (e.g.  EUR,  MXN).  
●  Currency  conversion  occurs  at  checkout  via  the  payment  provider.  
 ●  Internally,  all  transactions  are  recorded  only  in  USD.  
 
This  simplifies  reconciliation  and  regulatory  reporting.  
 
3.  Internal  Monetary  Units  
3.1  Buds  (Prepaid  Spending  Credits)  
Buds  are  WAIBY’s  internal  prepaid  spending  unit.  
●  1  USD  =  1  Bud  (fixed)  
 ●  Buds  are  prepaid  and  stored  in  the  user  account  
 
Key  characteristics:  
●  Buds  are  not  withdrawable  
 ●  Buds  are  not  refundable  to  the  original  payment  method  
 ●  Buds  are  usable  only  within  the  WAIBY  platform  
 
Purpose:  
●  Reduce  payment  friction  
 ●  Limit  chargeback  exposure  
 
●  Improve  transaction  security  
 
 
3.2  Wallet  Balance  (Income)  
Wallet  Balance  represents  withdrawable  USD  earnings .  
It  is  used  for:  
●  Creator  income  
 ●  Approved  internal  refunds  
 ●  Subscription  and  digital  item  earnings  
 
Wallet  Balance  is  strictly  separated  from  Buds.  
 
4.  Payment  Methods  (Deposits)  
Customers  may  purchase  Buds  using  the  following  regulated  methods:  
4.1  Card  Payments  
●  Visa  
 ●  Mastercard  
 
Characteristics:  
●  Instant  delivery  
 ●  Higher  chargeback  risk  
 ●  Subject  to  enhanced  fraud  monitoring  
 
4.2  PayPal  
●  Widely  trusted  global  method  
 ●  Dispute-capable  
 
●  Integrated  fraud  controls  
 
Cryptocurrency  deposits  are  not  supported  for  purchasing  Buds.  
 
5.  Deposit  Fees  &  Bonuses  
●  WAIBY  applies  0%  deposit  fees  
 ●  Users  receive  the  exact  USD  value  in  Buds  
 
Optional  incentives:  
●  First-deposit  bonuses  
 ●  Promotional  Bud  bonuses  
 
Bonuses:  
●  Are  non-withdrawable  
 ●  Are  subject  to  anti-abuse  controls  
 
 
6.  Orders,  Subscriptions  &  Funds  Handling  
6.1  One-Time  Orders  
●  Buds  are  deducted  from  the  customer  balance  
 ●  The  corresponding  USD  value  is  placed  into  escrow  
 ●  Funds  are  released  upon  order  completion,  subject  to  risk  controls  
 
6.2  Creator  Subscriptions  
●  Subscriptions  are  billed  on  a  recurring  basis  
 ●  Payment  is  collected  upfront  by  WAIBY  
 
●  Platform  fees  are  applied  immediately  
 ●  Net  earnings  are  credited  to  the  creator’s  Wallet  Balance  
 
Subscriptions  do  not  use  escrow,  as  access  is  granted  immediately.  
 
7.  Platform  Fees  
7.1  Creator  Platform  Fee  (Subscriptions,  Gifts  &  Wishlist  Items)  
A  10%  platform  fee  applies  to:  
●  Creator  subscriptions  
 ●  Wishlist  purchases  
 ●  Gifts  and  supporter  payments  
 
Example:  
 
Subscription
 
price:
 
$9.99
 
 
Platform
 
fee
 
(10%):
 
$1.00
 
 
Creator
 
Wallet
 
Balance
 
credited:
 
$8.99
 
Creators  always  see  net  earnings  in  their  transaction  history.  
 
7.2  Creator  Withdrawal  Fee  
Withdrawals  from  Wallet  Balance  are  subject  to  a  15%  withdrawal  fee .  
This  fee  covers:  
●  Payment  processing  
 ●  Fraud  and  chargeback  risk  
 ●  Platform  operations  
 
 
7.3  Cryptocurrency  Withdrawal  Surcharge  (Optional)  
Creators  may  optionally  withdraw  earnings  via  cryptocurrency  (USDT).  
●  Cryptocurrency  withdrawals  incur  an  additional  6%  processing  surcharge  
 ●  This  reflects:  
 ○  Blockchain  fees  
 ○  Volatility  exposure  
 ○  Compliance  and  operational  overhead  
 
Total  crypto  withdrawal  fees  may  therefore  be  higher  than  standard  payouts.  
 
8.  Withdrawals,  Holds  &  Risk  Controls  
8.1  Availability  Delays  
To  protect  the  platform  from  chargebacks  and  fraud:  
●  Withdrawals  are  not  instant  by  default  
 ●  Earnings  may  be  subject  to  time-based  availability  holds  
 ●  Hold  durations  vary  based  on  account  history  and  risk  signals  
 
8.2  Rolling  Reserves  
In  certain  cases,  a  portion  of  earnings  may  be  temporarily  held  as  a  rolling  reserve .  
●  Reserves  are  released  automatically  over  time  
 ●  Reserves  are  used  to  offset  confirmed  payment  disputes  when  necessary  
 
These  mechanisms  are  standard  marketplace  risk  controls.  
 
9.  Refund  Handling  
9.1  Internal  Refund  Destination  
All  approved  refunds  are  credited  to  the  user’s  Wallet  Balance .  
Refunds  are  not  returned  to  the  original  payment  method .  
9.2  External  Payment  Disputes  
If  a  user  initiates  a  chargeback  or  external  dispute:  
●  WAIBY  cooperates  with  the  payment  provider  
 ●  The  associated  account  may  be  restricted  or  terminated  
 ●  Internal  balances  may  be  frozen  to  prevent  abuse  
 
 
10.  Chargebacks  &  Abuse  Prevention  
Chargebacks  are  treated  as  high-risk  events .  
WAIBY  may  apply:  
●  Account  restrictions  
 ●  Deposit  limitations  
 ●  Withdrawal  holds  
 ●  Permanent  bans  in  cases  of  repeated  abuse  
 
In  some  cases,  account  restoration  may  require  a  one-time  non-withdrawable  digital  
purchase
 
to
 
offset
 
operational
 
risk.
 
 
11.  Tax  Handling  (High-Level)  
11.1  Marketplace  Tax  Position  
WAIBY  is  designed  to  operate  as  the  commercial  point  of  sale  for  customers.  
This  allows:  
●  Centralized  tax  handling  
 
●  Clear  customer  invoicing  
 ●  Reduced  compliance  burden  for  creators  
 
Creators  act  as  independent  service  providers  under  WAIBY’s  marketplace  framework.  
 
11.2  VAT  /  Sales  Tax  
Where  legally  required:  
●  Taxes  may  be  included  in  customer  pricing  
 ●  WAIBY  may  collect  and  remit  applicable  taxes  
 
Tax  treatment  depends  on  jurisdiction,  user  location,  and  applicable  laws.  
Final  implementation  is  aligned  with  legal  and  tax  advisors.  
 
12.  Auditability  &  Record  Keeping  
WAIBY  maintains  detailed  records  of:  
●  Payments  and  deposits  
 ●  Orders  and  subscriptions  
 ●  Fees,  holds,  and  reserves  
 ●  Refunds  and  disputes  
 ●  Payouts  and  withdrawals  
 
Records  are  retained  in  accordance  with  legal  and  regulatory  requirements.  
 
13.  Compliance  Philosophy  
WAIBY’s  payment  system  is  designed  to:  
●  Minimize  fraud  and  abuse  
 ●  Protect  customers  and  creators  
 ●  Meet  PSP  and  banking  requirements  
 ●  Scale  safely  across  jurisdictions  
 
Financial  clarity  and  traceability  are  treated  as  core  infrastructure,  not  optional  
features.
