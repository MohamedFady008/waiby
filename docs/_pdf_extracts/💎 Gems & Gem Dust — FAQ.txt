💎  Gems  &  Gem  Dust  —  FAQ  
What  are  Gems?  
Gems  are  bonus  rewards  you  earn  by  using  Waiby.  
They  are  not  cash  and  they  are  not  required  to  use  the  platform,  but  they  give  you  extra  
benefits
 
and
 
savings
.
 
You  can  earn  Gems  from:  
●  Completing  orders  
 ●  Daily  login  rewards  
 ●  Playing  games  and  activities  
 ●  Special  events  and  promotions  
 
 
What  is  Gem  Dust?  
Gem  Dust  is  a  smaller  reward  earned  mainly  from  games.  
Gem  Dust  is  collected  automatically  and  converted  into  Gems  once  you  have  enough.  
●  200  Gem  Dust  =  1  Gem  
 ●  Gem  Dust  cannot  be  withdrawn  
 ●  Gem  Dust  is  game-only  and  bonus-based  
 
Think  of  it  as  progress  toward  Gems.  
 
How  do  Gems  work?  
Gems  are  stored  in  your  Buff  Income  wallet.  
They  can  be  used  to:  
●  Buy  VIP  
 ●  Buy  frames,  cosmetics,  and  badges  
 ●  Reduce  the  price  of  certain  items  
 ●  Convert  into  Buds  and  withdraw  to  your  bank  (optional)  
 
 
How  much  are  Gems  worth?  
●  200  Gems  =  1  Bud  
 ●  1  Bud  =  1  USD  
 
Example:  
●  100  Gems  =  0.5  Bud  
 ●  When  withdrawing  to  your  bank,  a  15%  fee  applies  
 
 
Is  there  a  fee  when  using  Gems?  
●  ❌  No  fee  when  spending  Gems  inside  Waiby  
 ●  ✅  15%  fee  only  applies  when  withdrawing  to  your  bank  
 
This  means  Gems  are  more  valuable  when  used  on  the  platform.  
 
Can  I  withdraw  Gems  as  money?  
Yes,  but  it’s  optional.  
When  you  withdraw:  
1.  Gems  are  converted  into  Buds  
 
2.  A  15%  withdrawal  fee  is  applied  
 3.  The  remaining  amount  is  sent  to  your  bank  
 
Example:  
●  100  Gems  →  0.5  Bud  
 ●  After  fee  →  0.425  USD  
 
 
Can  I  use  Gems  to  buy  services  or  orders?  
❌  No.  
Gems  cannot  be  used  to:  
●  Buy  services  
 ●  Pay  for  orders  
 ●  Send  money  to  other  users  
 
They  are  designed  for  bonuses,  perks,  and  savings ,  not  direct  payments.  
 
How  do  discounts  with  Gems  work?  
Gems  can  be  used  to  reduce  the  price  of  certain  items.  
●  200  Gems  =  1  Bud  discount  
 ●  You  can  cover  up  to  50%  of  the  item  price  
 ●  The  final  price  will  always  be  greater  than  0  Buds  
 
Example  
Item  price:  9.99  Buds  
 
You
 
use
 
Gems
 
for
 
discount:
 
●  Max  discount:  4.99  Buds  
 ●  Remaining  price:  5.00  Buds  
 
This  helps  you  buy  items  even  if  you  don’t  have  enough  Buds.  
 
Can  Gems  be  transferred  to  other  users?  
❌  No.  
Gems  are  personal,  non-transferable,  and  tied  to  your  account.  
 
Do  Gems  expire?  
No.

Gems

Why  does  Waiby  use  Gems  and  Gem  Dust?  
Gems  and  Gem  Dust  are  designed  to:  
●  Reward  active  users  
 ●  Give  value  without  forcing  withdrawals  
 ●  Avoid  pay-to-win  mechanics  
 ●  Keep  the  marketplace  fair  and  safe  
 
You  can  fully  use  Waiby  without  ever  touching  Gems ,  but  they  give  you  extra  advantages  
if
 
you
 
do.
 
 
●  Gems  =  bonus  rewards  
 ●  Gem  Dust  =  progress  toward  Gems  
 
●  Spend  Gems  inside  Waiby  with  no  fees  
 ●  Withdraw  if  you  want  (15%  fee)  
 ●  No  pay-to-win,  no  forced  complexity
